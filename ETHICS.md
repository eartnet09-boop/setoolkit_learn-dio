# Ethical Use Statement

This repository documents a controlled cybersecurity lab exercise based on the Social-Engineer Toolkit (SET), created for an academic cybersecurity course. The content was produced in a laboratory environment with a controlled VM and local network isolation, under the explicit authorization of the instructor.

The techniques described here are intended only for systems and people with explicit written authorization. They must not be used against third parties, production systems, or any target outside the approved laboratory scope.

Using these techniques without authorization may violate applicable law in Brazil, including the Lei 12.737/2012 ("Lei Carolina Dieckmann"), the Marco Civil da Internet, and general criminal provisions. This repository is not legal advice, but the ethical and legal boundaries are clear: use only in authorized and supervised environments.

No real credentials, personal data, or victim-identifying information should appear in this repository. All screenshots, diagrams, and examples must be created from fictitious or self-owned assets in a lab environment.

This project is educational and documentation-focused: it describes the purpose, modules, and workflow of a social-engineering toolkit at the level of a technical report, without providing attack automation, payloads, or operational phishing materials suitable for real-world misuse.
